Blockfall – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Blockfall is a block puzzle game for Android, published on Google Play by Symxy. This policy explains what information is handled when you use it. The short version: the game itself collects nothing about you, and the only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising and payment services need in order to work.

Information the app itself collects

None. Blockfall has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics of its own and no servers. Your scores, coin balance and settings are stored on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app. Like most Android apps, that small settings file may also be included in your device's automatic Android backup to your own Google account, which you control in Android settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Blockfall shows advertisements served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers: your device's advertising ID, which you can reset or delete at any time in your Android settings, and the app set ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (the app never asks for location permission);
  • basic device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language, and this app's package name;
  • app interactions such as app launches, taps and video views, and how you interact with the ads shown;
  • diagnostic information about app performance, such as launch time, hang rate and energy usage.

This data is handled under Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Android's settings under Google → Ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, using Google's consent management platform. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the app.

Rewarded video advertisements are always optional: they are shown only when you choose to watch one.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (Remove Ads and coin packs) are processed entirely by Google Play. Blockfall never sees your payment details. Google Play provides the app with a purchase token so that the item can be delivered and, for Remove Ads, restored on a new device. Purchases are governed by the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

Blockfall requests internet access and network state, which are used only by the advertising and billing services described above. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

Blockfall is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app is listed for ages 13 and over.

Data deletion

Because the app stores nothing about you off your device, uninstalling it deletes all of its data. To limit advertising data, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Data collected by Google's advertising service is governed by Google's privacy controls at myadcenter.google.com.
